Meet Cannabis coin, a community working to put computing power to useful work.
We're building a community currency for efficient networks, with privacy at the heart of community claims.
There is no proof-of-work mining race, so participation does not depend on competitive hash grinding.
Ninety-nine percent of the supply is allocated to the community, with one percent for the founder, vested over twenty-four months.
Zero-knowledge proofs protect eligibility privacy, with one claim for each verified person when the public launch opens.
You can explore Cannabis coin, follow its development and help build the community.
