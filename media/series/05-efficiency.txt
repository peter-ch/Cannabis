Cannabis coin is built around efficient participation. There is no proof-of-work mining race and no competition to burn computation for new tokens.
The target network uses Ethereum proof-of-stake settlement. Useful computation happens separately, for tasks that requesters actually need and agree to pay for.
Training, rendering, analysis, simulation, and other software workloads can fit the architecture. The goal is productive computing, with measured costs and clear results. Bring a useful problem. Help build a useful network.
